# Iron Golem Armor

A small NeoForge mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 that makes vanilla Iron Golems and Snow Golems tougher. It's server-side: players can join with or without it, and with it they also see the snowball textures.

- **Iron golems:** armor, armor toughness and bonus health, for every golem or only player-built ones; fire resistance and immunities to poison, magma blocks, plants and powder snow; optional creeper hunting that keeps villagers out of the blast.
- **Snow golems:** armor, their own immunities including rain and water, an optional snow trail, and special snowballs: yellow ones (poison and nausea) and rock-filled ones (damage and knockback).
- **Live config:** works on existing worlds, and config changes apply without a restart.

**Requires:** Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.257 or newer. Download from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/golemarmor) or [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/hammerknife404/golemarmor/releases), and put the jar in the server's `mods/` folder, or your client's for singleplayer.
